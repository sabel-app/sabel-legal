User Agreement

Effective 4 November 2026

This agreement covers your use of Sabel on a device you control. Sabel lets you record scuba dives, attach photos or paper-log scans, maintain marine sightings, review local statistics, and manage a device-local session.

Local session

One-Tap Login opens a session stored on this device. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. You must agree to the Privacy Policy and this User Agreement before One-Tap Login is available. Log Out ends that session and leaves dive data in place. Delete Account removes the local session together with dive logs, sightings, media copies, and diagnostics on this install. Those deletes cannot be undone from Sabel.

Local records

You are responsible for the dive data and media you save. Sabel stores them on the device. There is no cloud backup in the app. If you delete the account or remove the app, the records on that install are gone.

Permissions

You can refuse the camera and still create and edit dive logs by typing values manually or importing images with the system photo picker. Camera access is requested only after you tap Scan / Snap or Take Photo. Notification permission uses only the system prompt after you enter the main tabs; you may refuse it without blocking the logbook. Sabel does not request tracking (ATT) permission.

Acceptable use

Do not use Sabel to break the law. The app does not provide medical, decompression, or dive-planning advice. Computer telemetry fields are for manual notes only and are not a substitute for a certified dive computer or training.

In-app purchases

Optional consumable Shells are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Prices shown in the store follow Apple billing. Shells unlock optional on-device features such as statistical export. Delete Account removes the local Shell balance on this install. Purchases are not transferable and are not restored as non-consumables.

Contact

Questions about this agreement can be sent through in-app Support.
