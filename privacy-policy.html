Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Sabel is a scuba dive logbook that runs on the iPhone you control. This policy explains what the app stores on that device, when it asks for hardware access, and how local sign-in works. Sabel does not upload your dive logs, sightings, or media to a Sabel server.

Local sign-in

One-Tap Login opens a session stored only on this device. Sabel keeps a device-local account identifier and a session token in app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. That identifier is not sent to a Sabel account server.

Log Out removes the session token. Dive logs and sightings stay on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier, the session token, dive logs, sightings, species notes, imported media copies, and local permission diagnostics from this install.

Data on this device

Dive parameters (site, depth, time, gas, ballast, notes), marine sightings, shop and guide ratings you enter, and photos or paper-log scans you attach are stored in the app sandbox. Optional export files such as a CSV summary are written only when you choose Export. Removing the app, or using Delete Account, deletes this local data.

Sample dive imagery shipped with the app is for illustration. It is not uploaded and is not treated as personal content you created.

Camera

Camera access is requested only after you choose an action that needs it, such as Scan / Snap a paper dive log or Take Photo on a dive or sighting screen. The camera runs only while the capture screen is open. Sabel does not use the camera in the background.

Photo library

Import uses the system photo picker. You choose individual images without granting Sabel full photo-library access. Selected photos are copied into the app sandbox for the related dive or sighting. Sharing a log card uses the system share sheet; Sabel does not request add-only photo-library permission of its own.

Tracking

After you sign in and reach the main tabs, iOS may show the system App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, Sabel reads the advertising identifier (IDFA) on this device to measure which features you open — Logs, Sightings, Stats, and Settings — so we can improve in-app guidance. Sabel does not sell your dive photos or share the identifier with third-party ad networks. If you deny tracking, Sabel clears any locally stored IDFA.

Notifications

After the tracking prompt finishes, iOS may show the system notification permission prompt. If you allow notifications, Sabel registers for remote notifications and stores the device token on this device so Apple can deliver alerts. There is no custom “allow notifications” screen in front of the system prompt.

Permissions Sabel does not request

Sabel does not request microphone, location, HealthKit, contacts, or Bluetooth permission. Camera capture is still photos only. Dive depth, duration, and temperature are entered manually.

Purchases

Sabel sells consumable Shells through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the charge. Shell balances are stored on this device only. Consumable Shells are not restored across devices. Refunds are handled by Apple. A small starter Shell balance may be granted locally when you first open the wallet on an install; it is not an App Store product.

What we do not collect

Sabel does not operate a Sabel analytics or backup server that receives your logbook. App Privacy is intended to reflect Data Not Collected for this local product.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through in-app Support.
